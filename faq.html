General Questions
Q. Are your services available online?
Yes. All our digital services are 100% online. You connect via WhatsApp or email, share your files or requirements, and receive the completed work — no physical visit required.
Q. Who can use KY Tech Services?
Our services are for everyone — students, job seekers, freelancers, small business owners, schools, startups, and enterprises. If you need affordable digital help, we are here for you.
Q. Can I contact you through WhatsApp?
Absolutely! WhatsApp is our primary channel. Send your requirement as text, voice note, photo, or document and our team will respond with pricing and next steps within minutes.
Q. Is KY Tech Services available across all of India?
Yes! We serve clients from Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Rajasthan, West Bengal, and all other states. Since everything is online, location is never a barrier.
Q. Do you support Marathi language communication?
Yes! We support both Marathi and English. Send your requirements in Marathi and we'll respond in your preferred language.
Q. What are your business hours?
Our primary support hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. You can send a WhatsApp message any time and we will reply during business hours.
Pricing & Payment
Q. What is the minimum price for your services?
Quick Digital Services start from as low as ₹99 for basic document formatting and data entry tasks. Website development packages start from ₹999.
Q. How is the final price decided?
We review your exact requirement and then provide a clear, all-inclusive quotation before any work begins. There are zero hidden charges — the price quoted is the price you pay.
Q. When do I need to make the payment?
For small tasks, full payment is required upfront. For larger projects (websites, e-commerce), we use milestone payments — typically 50% advance and 50% on delivery.
Q. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS/RTGS), and online payment links. Payment details are shared after quote confirmation.
Q. Do you offer discounts or combo packages?
Yes! We offer combo discounts for bundled services (e.g., website + logo + visiting card). Students, NGOs, and repeat clients also get special pricing. Ask on WhatsApp!
Q. Is there a refund policy?
We always share drafts/previews before final delivery. If we fail to deliver as agreed, we offer free revisions or a partial refund. Advance payments for fully completed work are non-refundable.
Website Development
Q. What types of websites do you build?
We build Static Business Websites, School & Academic Websites, Portfolio Websites, Landing Pages, and E-Commerce Websites — all mobile-responsive, SEO-friendly, and fast-loading.
Q. How long does website development take?
A standard static website: 3–7 working days. School/Academic websites: 5–10 days. E-commerce websites: 10–20 days depending on products and features required.
Q. Will my website be mobile-friendly?
Yes, every website is 100% responsive. It will look and work perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. Mobile-first design is standard in all our packages.
Q. Do you provide domain and hosting setup?
We assist you in purchasing a domain and hosting plan and help with full setup. Domain and hosting costs are billed separately at actual cost. We recommend trusted providers for best performance.
Q. Will my website rank on Google?
All websites are built with on-page SEO best practices — proper H1/H2 structure, meta tags, fast load speed, clean URLs, and schema markup. For ongoing Google ranking, we offer dedicated SEO services separately.
Q. Can I update my website myself after delivery?
Yes. We provide documentation on basic updates. For ongoing content changes, we offer affordable maintenance packages or per-edit charges on request.
Q. What do I need to provide to start website development?
Share your business name, logo (if available), list of pages needed, text/content, and reference website links you like. We'll guide you through everything on WhatsApp step by step.
Design Services
Q. What design services do you offer?
We offer Logo Design, Promotional Banner Design, Visiting Card / Business Card Design, Social Media Post Design, WhatsApp DP Design, and Advertisement Creatives — all at affordable prices.
Q. How many revisions are included?
Standard packages include 2 free revisions. Additional revisions are available at a small charge. We share a draft first before finalising any design.

Q. How long does logo or banner design take?
Logo design: 1–3 working days. Banners and visiting card designs: usually within 24 hours. Same-day delivery is available for urgent requests (small fee may apply).
Q. Can you design Instagram and Facebook social media posts?
Yes! We design eye-catching posts, stories, and promotional banners for Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn — perfectly sized for each platform.
